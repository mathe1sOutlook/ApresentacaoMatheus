Case 01 Joint

Media Portal — Digital repositioning

18+ years of operation · full redesign

Media Portal Soluções

Media & Broadcast · joint

Problem

A consolidated company — 18+ years, hundreds of clients, names like TV Cultura, MTV and CazéTV — with a digital presence far behind the maturity of the business.

Solution

Bruno structured the narrative, the institutional timeline and the product communication system; Matheus took the redesign into production — home, timeline, modules and the partner-ecosystem page — on a scope and schedule formalised in the first two weeks.

Value delivered

A digital presence that matches the brand's maturity, ready to attract and convert new corporate contracts.

Stack

  • TypeScript
  • Supabase
  • Vercel

Numbers

  • 2 weeks of scope definition

Case 03 Validated case · X

Ame × X (Twitter)

28.3M impressions

Ame Digital × X (Twitter)

Fintech · case validated by X · Bruno

Problem

The fintech needed to plug into culturally relevant conversations — a reality show, Pride Month — and earn brand relevance on the platform.

Solution

Media and content strategy across the #AMEnoDFCEX and #AmeSemModeração campaigns, with Promoted Trends and First View.

Value delivered

+28.3M impressions, +600K engagements (8% rate), mentions 900% and 520% above benchmark across the two campaigns — results published as an official X success case.

Formats

  • Promoted Trend
  • First View
  • Always-on content

Numbers

  • 28.3M impressions
  • 600K engagements · 8% rate
  • 900% / 520% mentions above benchmark

Method
