Real challenges

Does your operation recognise itself here?

Common situations in companies that want to grow but hit obstacles in practice. If any of these feels familiar, you are not alone.

Marketing

Spend goes out every month, yet it is hard to tell which actions actually generate opportunities and sales.

  • Campaigns, content and events with no source tracking
  • Budget set with little grounding in historical data
Processes

More clients, more people and more demands — with processes still running as they did when the company was smaller.

  • Information concentrated in a few people
  • Each team runs on its own spreadsheets and methods
Communication

Website, sales team and communications present the company in ways that are not always aligned.

  • Sales proposals disconnected from the digital positioning
  • Clients struggle to clearly understand the offer
Data

Different sources show different results, making analysis slow and unreliable.

  • Indicators consolidated by hand
  • Decisions made before the information is organised
Technology

The company invests in tools, but the operation still depends on parallel processes and manual controls.

  • Systems that force the operation to adapt to the tool
  • Spreadsheets, messages and side controls to cover limitations
Continuity

The solution goes live, but improvements, adjustments and follow-up stop happening after delivery.

  • No maintenance or ongoing follow-up
  • Loose ends piling up after launch

Technology & Data

Data analysis

Beyond dashboards: models that reveal patterns a standard report does not show. Self-organizing maps (SOM) to segment customers and products with no prior categories; prototype-based classification (LVQ) to place each customer in known profiles — risk, default, behavior; and adaptive networks (ART) that learn from data arriving every day without forgetting what they already knew.

You receive
Live dashboards, customer segmentation and risk profiles that update with the operation.
Technology & Data

Automation & integrations

ERP, CRM, spreadsheets and tools talking to each other through APIs and automated routines. Data is typed once and lands where it is needed — no rework, no manual copying, no two versions of the same information.

You receive
Integrations in production, monitored, with alerts when something stops.
Technology & Data

AI applied to the business

Forecasting and decisions with models chosen by the problem, not by fashion. Radial basis function networks (RBF) to estimate sales, costs and cash flow from history; incremental learning (ART) for operations whose data changes continuously; and associative memory (Hopfield) to reconstruct incomplete patterns. Always with accuracy measured on your own data — not promises.

You receive
Model in use in the operation, continuously evaluated, with an accuracy report.
